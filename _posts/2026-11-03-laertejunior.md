# Laerte Junior

![Laerte Junior](/assets/images/laertejunior.jpg)

A proud son of Brazil, Laerte Junior was a true master of PowerShell and SQL Server, an inspiring mentor, and a radiant soul who poured his heart into sharing his knowledge with the community. Beyond his remarkable technical talent, he was a devoted person, a loving guy a cherished friend whose kindness and light will be deeply missed by all who had the privilege of knowing him.

## Thoughts and Memories from Friends

Laerte was a true friend—the kind of person who would never let anyone down, no matter the circumstances. I first met him in Seattle in 2012 at the SQL PASS conference, and from that moment on, his loyalty, warmth, and generous spirit left a lasting impression on me. Whether you needed help or just someone to lean on, he was always there without hesitation. My heart is heavy, and I will deeply miss having such a genuine and dependable soul in this world. – Alexandre Araujo
